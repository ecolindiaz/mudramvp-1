## Content Rules (compact)

Prioritize clarity, authority, and user intent. Ship short, verifiable improvements weekly.

### FAQ quality & coverage
- Goal: cover genuine high-intent questions with concise answers.
- Rules:
  - Normalize & dedupe questions; avoid synonyms/near-duplicates.
  - Target 8–20 FAQs on core pages (home, product, pricing, implementation).
  - Answers: 1–3 sentences; add short lists only when helpful.
  - If FAQs exist on the page, add matching FAQPage JSON-LD; keep answers in sync.

### Testimonials & authority signals
- Add short, specific testimonials and recognizable logos (with permission).
- Prefer quotes that state outcomes, metrics, or named roles/brands.

### Entities and terminology
- Use consistent names for product, features, and entities; define terms once.
- Avoid marketing jargon that reduces precision; prefer concrete nouns and verbs.

### Answer-first writing
- Start sections with a clear summary; follow with details and links.
- Convert dense paragraphs to lists/tables where possible.

### Freshness (foundation for future scoring)
- Surface last updated info on docs/pricing pages; keep key pages updated quarterly.
- Reflect recent product changes; update FAQs when features or pricing change.

### Verification mindset
- Every recommendation should be checkable via crawl (DOM or JSON-LD).
- Keep changes small enough to ship this week; revisit next week.
